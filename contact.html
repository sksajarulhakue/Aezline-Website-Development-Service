Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and process.

How long does it take to complete a website?

The timeline for website development varies depending on the complexity and scope of the project. A simple website might take 2-4 weeks, while more complex projects with custom functionality could take 8-12 weeks or more. During our initial consultation, we'll provide you with a more accurate timeline based on your specific requirements.

What information do you need to start a project?

To get started, we typically need information about your business, target audience, project goals, design preferences, and any specific functionality requirements. We'll guide you through this process during our initial planning phase to ensure we have all the necessary details to create a successful website for your business.

Do you provide website maintenance services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive website maintenance packages to keep your site secure, up-to-date, and performing optimally. Our maintenance services include regular updates, security monitoring, performance optimization, content updates, and technical support. We recommend ongoing maintenance for all websites to ensure they continue to function properly and remain secure.

What is your payment structure?

Our standard payment structure is 50% of the project cost upfront to secure your spot in our development schedule, with the remaining 50% due upon project completion before the website goes live. For larger projects, we may establish a milestone-based payment schedule. We accept payments via bank transfer, credit card, and other secure payment methods.
