Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and pricing

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including bank transfers, UPI, PayPal, and major credit/debit cards. We can also accommodate other payment methods based on your preference.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for larger projects. Typically, we require a 50% advance payment to begin work, with the remaining 50% due upon project completion.

How long does it take to complete a website?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity and scope. A simple landing page might take 1-2 weeks, while a custom e-commerce website could take 4-8 weeks. During our initial consultation, we'll provide you with a detailed timeline based on your specific requirements.

Do you provide domain and hosting services?

Yes, we can help you register a domain name and set up hosting for your website. We work with reliable hosting providers to ensure your website has excellent uptime and performance.

Can I update the website myself after it's built?

Absolutely! We build websites with user-friendly content management systems that allow you to easily update content, add new pages, and make basic changes without technical knowledge. We also provide training to help you get comfortable with managing your website.

Do you offer website maintenance services?

Yes, we offer ongoing maintenance packages to keep your website secure, up-to-date, and performing optimally. Our maintenance services include regular updates, security monitoring, backup management, and technical support.
